# EDUARDORB WORLD — Design System

Brand: EDUARDORB WORLD. Tagline: Intelligence · Software · Innovation.

Palette: #080E19 background, #101A2B surface, #60A5FA blue, #67E8F9 cyan, #F1F5FB foreground. Use system sans-serif, generous spacing, accessible contrast and reduced-motion support. Product names remain distinct; do not alter client branding.

The portfolio favicon is located in `docs/favicon.svg`.
